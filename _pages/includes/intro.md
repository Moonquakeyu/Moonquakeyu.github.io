I am **Peiang (Ignatius) Miao**, an MSc student in Metaverse Technology at [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/). My research focuses on multimodal agents, vision-language models, evidence-grounded reasoning, efficient and cost-aware inference, physical-world visual intelligence, digital dentistry, and clinical decision-support AI.

At PolyU, I work with [Dr. Dun Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=UWUW3ogAAAAJ&hl=en)  on industrial visual intelligence. I also collaborate with [Prof. Junyu Shi](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Junyu-Shi-3) on clinical-dental AI and computational biology.

I earned a BE and BSc in Software Engineering through the joint University College Dublin and Beijing University of Technology programme, graduating with Upper Second Class Honours and the programme's Best Final Year Project award. My undergraduate research was conducted with [Associate Prof. Soumyabrata Dev](https://soumyabrata.dev/).
